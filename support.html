Akshi Support

Get started

  1. Mount the phone on your cane or a chest strap, rear camera facing forward and tilted down toward the floor about two steps ahead.
  2. Press Start. On LiDAR iPhones, Akshi warns about steps and obstacles straight away.
  3. Add a place. Places → Add a place, then add landmarks: doorways, rooms, and hazards such as a step down.
  4. Print the markers (Print or save markers) and tape each one about a stride before its spot, low on a wall, door frame or baseboard, flat and well lit. Give each step a marker on both sides.
  5. Where am I? repeats the last marker. You can assign it to the Action button: iPhone Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → Akshi → Where am I?

Try it without setting anything up

In Akshi, open Places → Try the sample place, then print the four sample markers (PDF) or show them on another screen. Press Start and point the camera at marker 1: Akshi says "Front door." Marker 3 plays the hazard tone and says "Step down. Living room."

Common questions

It says "Step detection needs a LiDAR iPhone." Your iPhone has no LiDAR sensor. Markers still work; steps and obstacles are not detected, so rely on your cane.

It says "Cannot see the floor. Tilt the phone down." The camera is aimed too flat. Tilt it down until the warning stops.

A marker isn't read. Make sure it's flat, well lit, not glossy, and facing the direction you approach from. Printing larger helps.

"Guidance stopped. Use your cane." The camera stopped delivering images (the phone locked, a call arrived, or it got too hot). Akshi says "Guidance resumed" when it recovers.

Akshi supplements your cane, guide dog and the people around you. It can miss steps, obstacles or markers. Do not rely on it in an emergency.
