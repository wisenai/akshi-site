Akshi Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Applies to Akshi for iPhone, version 1.0 and later

Summary

Akshi does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It makes no internet connections.

Camera

Akshi uses the iPhone's rear camera and, where available, its LiDAR depth sensor to read the markers you place and to detect steps and obstacles. Camera images and depth data are processed on your iPhone as they arrive and are then discarded. They are never saved, uploaded or shared.

Your places and settings

The places, landmark names and notes you enter, and your settings, are saved only on your iPhone, inside Akshi's own storage. They are included in your iPhone's normal backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; Akshi's developer cannot see them. Deleting Akshi deletes them.

Sharing a place

If you choose "Share this place", Akshi creates a file with that place's landmark list and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Messages, AirDrop, email, Files) is your choice and is governed by that service.

Developer mode

If you turn on Developer mode, Akshi records depth measurements from your walks into files on your iPhone for tuning. These recordings contain numbers, not images, and stay on the device unless you copy them off yourself.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

Akshi contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs.

Children

Akshi collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this — for example an optional online feature — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will ask for your permission first.

Contact
