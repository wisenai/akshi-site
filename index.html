Akshi: Indoor Guide

Akshi — "eye" in Sanskrit — is an iPhone app that helps people who are blind or have low vision move around the indoor places they know.

  • Talking markers. Name the doorways, rooms and hazards in your home or office, print a marker for each from the app, and tape them up. Akshi speaks each one as you walk past.
  • Step and obstacle warnings. On iPhones with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models), Akshi watches the floor ahead and warns "Step down", "Step up" or beeps for obstacles — no setup needed.
  • Private. Everything runs on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no data collected.

Akshi supplements your cane, guide dog and the people around you. It can miss things and is not a medical device. Do not rely on it in an emergency.
